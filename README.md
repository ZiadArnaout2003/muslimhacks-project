# Al-Noor International School Platform

**Al-Noor International School** is an online school that combines **academic education with Islamic education** in one platform.

The platform connects students and families with qualified teachers, provides online courses and live learning, and offers support for families who may not be able to afford the full cost of education.

## What Al-Noor Offers

###  Education

Students can discover and enroll in courses across different subjects, including:

* Mathematics
* Physics
* Islamic Studies
* And other subjects

Students have their own learning space where they can follow their courses, complete assignments, take quizzes, and track their progress.

###  Opportunities for Teachers

Qualified teachers can apply to teach through the platform.

Teachers can:

* Create a professional profile
* Submit their application and qualifications
* Manage their availability
* Teach students online
* Manage their courses and classes
* Grade assignments and follow student progress

This allows Al-Noor to create opportunities for qualified educators while giving students access to quality teaching.

###  Financial Assistance

Al-Noor is designed to make education accessible to families who may not be able to afford the full cost of courses.

Families can apply for financial assistance, and approved support can reduce the cost of their education.

###  Family & Student Experience

Parents can manage their children's education from one place, including:

* Registering and managing children
* Finding teachers and courses
* Booking classes
* Managing enrollments
* Viewing payments and invoices
* Tracking their children's learning

Students have a dedicated space for their own courses, assignments, quizzes, and progress.

###  Islamic Education & Oversight

Islamic education is an important part of Al-Noor.

The platform includes support for Islamic Studies courses and a scholar/reviewer role intended to help oversee Islamic educational content.

###  Built for an International Community

Al-Noor is designed with an international audience in mind, with support for:

* English
* Arabic
* French
* Right-to-left Arabic interfaces

## The Vision

Al-Noor aims to bring together **education, opportunity, and accessibility**.

The goal is simple:

> **Give students access to quality education, give qualified teachers opportunities to teach, and help families in need access education they might otherwise not be able to afford.**

## Technology

The platform is built using modern web technologies, including **React, TypeScript, and Supabase**.

The system includes separate experiences for students, parents, teachers, administrators, and scholars, with appropriate access and permissions for each role.

## Project Status

Al-Noor currently includes the core functionality needed to demonstrate the platform's main experience, including student learning, teacher applications, course management, bookings, assignments, quizzes, financial assistance, and administrative management.

Some features, such as messaging, live video integration, and real payment processing, are currently prepared for future development.
